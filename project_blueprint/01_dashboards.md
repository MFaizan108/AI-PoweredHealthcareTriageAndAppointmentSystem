# 1. Dashboards

Main 5 major dashboards:

## 1. 👤 Patient Dashboard

Patient ke liye:

- Profile
- Health information
- Symptom assessment
- AI triage results
- Recommended department
- Appointment booking
- Upcoming appointments
- Appointment history
- Prescriptions
- Medical records
- Lab reports
- Notifications
- Messages with doctor
- Emergency guidance
- Feedback/reviews

## 2. 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Dashboard

Doctor ko sabse important dashboard milega:

- Today's appointments
- Upcoming appointments
- Patient queue
- Patient profile
- Patient medical history
- AI triage summary
- Symptoms submitted by patient
- Risk/urgency level
- Consultation notes
- Diagnosis entered by doctor
- Prescription management
- Lab test recommendations
- Follow-up scheduling
- Appointment status
- Patient communication

Doctor ke dashboard par example:

```
Today's Appointments: 12
Completed: 7
Waiting: 3
Cancelled: 2

AI Triage:
🔴 High Priority: 1
🟠 Moderate: 4
🟢 Low: 7
```

## 3. 🏥 Hospital/Admin Dashboard

Ye project ka main management dashboard hoga.

Admin:

- Total patients
- Total doctors
- Departments
- Appointments
- Active appointments
- Cancelled appointments
- Revenue
- Doctor performance
- Patient statistics
- AI triage statistics
- Emergency cases
- Reports
- User management
- Role management
- Audit logs
- System settings

Charts:

- Patients per Month
- Appointments per Month
- Emergency Cases
- Department-wise Patients
- Doctor-wise Appointments

## 4. 🧑‍💼 Receptionist Dashboard

Receptionist:

- Register patient
- Search patient
- Book appointment
- Reschedule appointment
- Cancel appointment
- Check doctor availability
- Manage waiting queue
- Check-in patient
- Mark patient as arrived
- Generate appointment token

Example:

```
Token   Patient       Doctor          Status
A-101   Ali           Dr. Ahmed       Waiting
A-102   Sara          Dr. Ahmed       In Consultation
A-103   Hamza         Dr. Ahmed       Completed
```

## 5. 🧪 Lab Staff Dashboard

Lab staff:

- Test requests
- Pending tests
- Sample status
- Upload reports
- Update test results
- Patient lab history

Doctor ko automatically lab report available ho jaye.
